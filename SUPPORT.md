# Support

Mudrava RUM is free and open source. Support is provided on a best-effort basis
through these channels:

- **WordPress.org support forum** (preferred, public):
  [wordpress.org/support/plugin/mudrava-rum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/mudrava-rum/)
  - public threads help other site owners and improve our search visibility.
- **Email:** [support@mudrava.com](mailto:support@mudrava.com) for anything
  private.
- **Security issues:** do not open a public issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Before you open a ticket

Performance questions need context, so please include:

1. WordPress, PHP and plugin versions, and your host.
2. Whether the Live Monitor shows any rows at all (a collector that records
   nothing is a different bug from wrong numbers).
3. Your sampling rate setting and whether a page cache or CDN sits in front.
4. For "no data" reports: whether cron is firing and whether the summary email
   arrived.

## Response times

We aim to acknowledge public forum threads and email within 2-3 business days.
There is no guaranteed support SLA for the free plugin.

## Commercial help

For tuning, retention policy, or rolling RUM out across many sites, reach out at
[mudrava.com/en/](https://mudrava.com/en/).
